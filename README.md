## About

2474G Gladiators is a competitive Robotics team within 2474 Walnut Grove Robotics. We design, build, program, and continuously improve robots for competition while developing our engineering, problem-solving, and leadership skills.

This repository contains the software, documentation, and resources developed by Team 2474G throughout the season.

## What We Do

* Design and build competitive robots
* Develop autonomous and driver-control systems
* Test, iterate, and optimize performance
* Apply engineering principles to real-world challenges
* Work together to compete at the highest level possible

## Technologies

* VEX V5
* C++
* Autonomous Programming
* Motion Control
* CAD & Engineering Design

## Team Philosophy

We believe great robots are built through:

* Continuous improvement
* Attention to detail
* Effective teamwork
* Creative problem solving
* Dedication to excellence
